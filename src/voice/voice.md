## What I will not claim

- No salaries, placement figures, earnings, job guarantees or hiring outcomes.
- No student testimonials or student identities.
- No partnerships, partner logos, certifications or accreditations.
- No reach, enrolment or other metrics.
- No statistic or dated claim without an approved source in the claim ledger.
- No fabricated product interfaces: tool demos use genuine screen captures only.
- Never imply a synthetic voice is a real person (brief §08).

Any of these can appear only if supplied as an approved fact.

## My format

- Short: 45–75 s, 9:16. Hook by 3 s, relevance by 10 s, one worked example, one practical
  takeaway, one simple CTA.
- Long: 3–5 min, 16:9. Hook + outcome by 15 s, context and prerequisites to 45 s, demo or
  step-by-step build to 3:30, then recap, one limitation and the next action; close with the CTA,
  sources and the next learning path.
- Default CTA: "Explore AI learning at ziro.digital".
- Synthetic narration, no presenter on camera.
