## Who I am talking to

One person: an Indian student, early-career developer or career switcher who has used a chatbot,
has heard "AI agents" everywhere, and wants to build something real for their portfolio — not read
more AI news.

Priya, a 3rd-year B.Tech CSE student at a tier-2 college in India. She has built a to-do app and
one mini-project using the ChatGPT API. She is applying for internships and has had no replies
yet. She is stuck on: "everyone's portfolio looks the same — what do I build that actually stands
out?" She watches tech Shorts on her phone at night.

## How I actually talk

Clear, credible, practical, energetic without hype. Show the real demonstration and the learning
outcome; explain one idea at a time; prefer a worked example over a definition list.

The brand is written "Ziro" and spoken "ZAIRO".

No Ziro transcripts exist yet; these sample lines define the delivery:

- "Let's build it and see."
- "Here's the part most tutorials skip."
- "You don't need more theory — you need one working project."

Spoken style: short sentences, direct address ("you"), one idea per sentence, pauses instead of
filler words, Indian-English phrasing, no slang.

## Words I never use

Hype words: "game-changer", "revolutionary", "insane", "mind-blowing", "unlock", "secret",
"ultimate", "dive in", "let's get into it".

Also never: "crack the interview", "100% guaranteed", "bro", "guys", "roadmap to success",
"trust me", "easy money", "in just X days", "nobody will tell you this", "watch till the end".

## Words I do use

Recurring: "build something real", "your next project", "one step, done well".

- Long-video sign-off: "That's your next build. Explore AI learning at ziro.digital."
- Short-format CTA stays the bare line: "Explore AI learning at ziro.digital."

## What I will not claim

- No salaries, placement figures, earnings, job guarantees or hiring outcomes.
- No student testimonials or student identities.
- No partnerships, partner logos, certifications or accreditations.
- No reach, enrolment or other metrics.
- No statistic or dated claim without an approved source in the claim ledger.
- No fabricated product interfaces: tool demos use genuine screen captures only.
- Never imply a synthetic voice is a real person (brief §08).

Any of these can appear only if supplied as an approved fact.

## My format

- Short: 45–75 s, 9:16. Hook by 3 s, relevance by 10 s, one worked example, one practical
  takeaway, one simple CTA.
- Long: 3–5 min, 16:9. Hook + outcome by 15 s, context and prerequisites to 45 s, demo or
  step-by-step build to 3:30, then recap, one limitation and the next action; close with the CTA,
  sources and the next learning path.
- Default CTA: "Explore AI learning at ziro.digital".
- Synthetic narration, no presenter on camera.

- CTA wording confirmed: "Explore AI learning at ziro.digital".
- Narrator: female, Indian-English accent; the final voice is chosen by testing in Phase 4P.
- No intro sting.
- End card: yes, about 2 seconds, Ziro logo + ziro.digital.
